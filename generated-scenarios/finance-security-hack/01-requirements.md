## ⚡ Non-Functional Requirements (NFRs)

Security and Operational Excellence take priority over minimizing component count.
Use synthetic educational content only; no real customer records, portfolio data or trade execution.
Disable local/shared-key authentication wherever the selected services support it.
Enforce authorization as well as private connectivity: private networking alone is not Zero Trust.
Separate deployment permissions from runtime permissions and scope access to the required resources and operations.
Record diagnostics with retention and redaction, explicit operational failures, and a recoverable deployment procedure.

The word "high-scale" does not supply measurable acceptance thresholds.
Concurrent chats, requests per minute, token limits, latency percentiles, availability, test duration
and spending limits are deployment-time sizing inputs; their absence does not block local construction after user clarification.
No production readiness, compliance certification, model capacity, cost estimate, or availability claim is made.
