# Adatum Corporation synthetic brokerage education source

This is invented training policy, not a statement about a real business or current market conditions.
Version: 2026-10-22. Educational only; not personalized investment advice.

## FAQ-01 Market orders
A market order prioritizes execution over a specified price. The execution price can differ from the last quoted price.
Execution is not guaranteed when trading is halted or liquidity is unavailable.

## FAQ-02 Limit orders
A buy limit order sets the highest acceptable purchase price; a sell limit sets the lowest acceptable sale price.
A limit order may never execute. This reference does not place orders.

## FAQ-03 Investing risk
Investments can lose value, including principal. No return is guaranteed.
Diversification spreads exposure but does not eliminate investment risk.

## FAQ-04 Brokerage role
Adatum Corporation is represented as an investment brokerage in this synthetic reference, not a retail bank.
The assistant provides education and cannot hold deposits, execute trades, or access customer accounts.

## FAQ-05 Synthetic fees
The synthetic education reference lists a hypothetical assisted-service fee of 5 training units per request.
Training units are not money. This is not a real price list or a quote for any transaction.

## FAQ-06 Settlement
Settlement is the exchange of cash and securities after a trade. Timing depends on market, instrument and jurisdiction.
No specific settlement deadline is promised by this FAQ.

## FAQ-07 Volatility
Volatility describes price variation over time. Large price moves can increase risk and affect order execution.
Historical performance is not a guarantee of future results.

## FAQ-08 Margin
Borrowing to invest can magnify losses and can lead to additional collateral requirements or forced liquidation.
No margin account or lending feature exists in this training reference.

## FAQ-09 Privacy
Do not submit names, account numbers, credentials, portfolio positions or other personal data to this assistant.
There is no customer-account lookup function. Request human support through an approved organizational channel.

## FAQ-10 Advice boundary
The assistant cannot recommend buying or selling an individual security or assess personal investment suitability.
It can explain general concepts from this source and recommend seeking an appropriately qualified professional.

## FAQ-11 Unsupported questions
Current security prices, tax advice, live execution status and account balances are outside this source.
The assistant must say that the approved FAQ does not contain the answer.

## FAQ-12 Incident handling
If an answer fails or seems inaccurate, retain its correlation identifier and contact the training operator.
A signed response receipt proves integrity of answer bytes, not factual correctness or regulatory approval.
