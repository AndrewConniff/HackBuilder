### Recommended security controls

- Managed identity and scoped RBAC for Azure service calls; disable shared-key authentication.
- Salted framework password hashes, durable local accounts and persistent data-protection keys.
- Secure HttpOnly cookies, 30-minute non-sliding session, five failed logins then 15-minute lockout.
- Antiforgery validation on mutations, server-assigned roles and optimistic concurrency.
- Unique generated deployment passwords in separate gitignored private access notes; no live credentials in exports.
- TLS 1.2 minimum, encryption at rest, diagnostics without passwords, tokens or material bodies.
- Separate deploying-human full resource/data administration from narrow workload permissions.
