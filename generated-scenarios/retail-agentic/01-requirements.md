### Proposed deterministic policies

These are design defaults, not additional user answers:

- One warehouse, up to 20 lines per cart, quantity 1-10, maximum order total USD 200.
- Persist integer cents and one currency. No model-generated arithmetic or payment credentials.
- Reject substitutions with conflicting or missing allergen/dietary metadata; never infer that food is safe.
- A proposed replacement includes product ID, quantity, total and delivery slot in its reviewed fingerprint.
- Customer approval is valid for 10 minutes and the exact customer/order/proposal version only.
- Expired, rejected or stale proposals leave the affected action uncommitted and the order on a visible hold.
- Stock changes require a fresh proposal/confirmation if they invalidate the accepted version.
- No charge increase, replacement or slot change is implicitly authorized by the initial order confirmation.
- Simulated payment and delivery adapters have explicit simulation labels in every receipt.
